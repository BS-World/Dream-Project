FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

Here are answers to the most common questions about our remote internship: work mode, duration, eligibility, and certificates. Please read this once before applying.

If you still have any questions, you can always reach us via the Contact Us page or WhatsApp.

Alfido Tech's Remote IT Internship is a structured online program where you work on real-world tasks with guided resources and basic mentor support. You can complete all tasks from your own laptop/PC at home – there is no need to visit any physical office.

Yes, the internship is 100% remote / work from home. All tasks, submissions, meetings and communication are done online. You only need a stable internet connection, a laptop/PC, and basic tools like VS Code, Google Drive and GitHub.

The typical duration is between 1 to 3 months, depending on the domain. Your Offer Letter clearly mentions the duration, tasks and submission deadlines. We can also help you align the duration with your college requirements if needed.

This internship is primarily learning-focused and training-based, where you work on real projects and tasks. In some cases, performance-based opportunities or referrals may be provided, but by default there is no guaranteed stipend. The main focus is your skills, projects, and a verified certificate.

Yes. Interns who successfully complete their tasks receive an official Alfido Tech Internship Certificate. The certificate includes your name, domain, duration and completion date. It can also be verified online using our Certificate Verification Portal.

Any student or fresher interested in IT, Computer Science, Data, Design, Marketing or related domains can apply. B.Tech, B.Sc, BCA, MCA, Diploma, MBA students and working professionals are welcome. For some domains (like Data Science or ML), basic programming knowledge is helpful but not always mandatory.

Applying is simple:
  1. Visit the official Alfido Tech website and open the Internship Registration page.
  2. Choose your domain and fill your basic details and WhatsApp number correctly.
  3. Submit the form. You will receive updates about your Offer Letter and next steps on your registered contact details.
For any issues during registration, you can contact our support team via WhatsApp or the Contact page.
