Cookie Policy

Last Updated: 07 December 2025

This Cookie Policy explains how Alfido Tech (“we”, “our”, “us”) uses cookies and similar technologies when you visit our website https://www.alfidotech.com (“Website”).

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device (computer, tablet, smartphone) when you visit a website. They help the site remember your actions and preferences over time.

2. Types of Cookies We Use

2.1 Essential Cookies

These cookies are necessary for the Website to function properly. Without them, certain features may not be available.

2.2 Analytics & Performance Cookies

These cookies help us understand how visitors use our Website, which pages are most visited, and how users interact with content. This information is used to improve the Website and user experience.

2.3 Preference Cookies

These cookies store your preferences (such as language settings or saved information) so you do not have to re-enter them each time.

2.4 Advertising & Third-Party Cookies (if applicable)

We may use third-party services (such as Google services) that place cookies to show relevant advertisements or measure ad performance. These third parties may collect information about your activity across different websites.

3. How We Use Cookies

  • To enable certain Website features.
  • To remember your preferences.
  • To analyze traffic and usage patterns.
  • To improve the performance and user experience of the Website.

4. Managing Cookies

Most web browsers allow you to control cookies through their settings. You can choose to:

  • Block all cookies.
  • Block third-party cookies.
  • Delete cookies when you close the browser.

However, if you block or delete certain cookies, some features of the Website may not function correctly.
