Refund Policy

Last Updated: 07 December 2025

At Alfido Tech, we aim to provide quality services including internships, training programs, and related offerings. This Refund Policy explains when refunds may be provided for payments made on our Website https://www.alfidotech.com.

1. General Policy

By making a payment for any service, you acknowledge and agree to the terms of this Refund Policy.

2. Non-Refundable Services

The following payments are generally non-refundable:

  • Internship fees.
  • Training or course fees.
  • Certificate processing or verification charges.
  • Any service already delivered in full or in significant part.

3. Eligible Refund Cases

A refund may be considered only under the following conditions:

  • Duplicate payment made for the same service.
  • Payment was made by mistake (for example, wrong amount or wrong service selected), and the service has not yet been started or used.
  • Technical error on our payment gateway resulting in an incorrect charge.

4. How to Request a Refund

To request a refund, you must contact us within a reasonable period from the date of payment and provide:

  • Full name
  • Registered email address
  • Transaction ID or payment reference
  • Date and amount of payment
  • Reason for refund request

Please send your request to:
Email: support@alfidotech.com

5. Refund Processing

Once we receive your refund request, we will review it and notify you of the approval or rejection. If approved, the refund will be processed to the original method of payment within a commercially reasonable timeframe. The exact time depends on your bank or payment provider.

6. No Cash Refunds

Refunds, if approved, will generally be processed only to the original payment method. We do not issue cash refunds.

7. Changes to This Refund Policy

We may update this Refund Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with the updated “Last Updated” date.
