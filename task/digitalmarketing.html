Digital Marketing — Internship Tasks

Beginner Tasks + Alfido Tech-focused Digital Marketing Projects

Objective

Provide interns with hands-on experience in digital marketing by creating real promotional strategies, campaigns, and content specifically for **Alfido Tech**. These tasks help interns understand how marketing works in real businesses.

Features

  • SEO planning and keyword research
  • Social media content creation
  • Brand marketing for Alfido Tech
  • Email marketing fundamentals
  • Marketing analytics and reporting

Tools

Canva Google Analytics Google Trends Meta Business Suite LinkedIn Mailchimp

Beginner Level Tasks

  • Study Alfido Tech's website & list its key services.
  • Create 3 social posts explaining "What Alfido Tech does" (use Canva).
  • Identify 10 trending keywords related to internships & tech services in India.
  • Write a short competitor analysis (2–3 competitors).

Note: Out of the 4 main tasks below, you must complete any 3 tasks.

Tasks (4)

Goal

Create a week-long content plan to promote Alfido Tech's internships.

Requirements
  • Design 5–7 Canva posts about internships/services.
  • Create captions + relevant hashtags.
  • Provide a posting schedule (dates & times).
Deliverables
  1. Google Drive/Canva link to designs
  2. Content calendar (PDF/DOC)

Goal

Create an SEO plan for improving Alfido Tech’s website visibility.

Requirements
  • Identify 15–20 high-value keywords (Internship + IT services).
  • Prepare 3 blog topic suggestions.
  • Create a sample blog (300–400 words).
Deliverables
  1. Keyword research sheet
  2. Blog article + topic list

Goal

Design a 20–40 sec video promoting Alfido Tech internships or services.

Requirements
  • Use Canva/CapCut for editing.
  • Add text overlays + transitions.
  • Include Alfido Tech branding (Logo + Colors).
Deliverables
  1. Video link (Drive/YouTube)
  2. Thumbnail image (PNG/JPG)

Goal

Create a simple email campaign to promote Alfido Tech internships.

Requirements
  • Design 1 promotional email (HTML or Canva template).
  • Write subject line + preview text.
  • Describe the target audience.
Deliverables
  1. Email template file/link
  2. Audience segmentation notes

How to Submit Your Tasks

  1. For each task:
    • Create a separate document (DOC/PDF) with screenshots, links, and explanations.
  2. Upload files:
    • Upload designs/videos/blogs to Google Drive or GitHub.
    • Ensure public viewing permissions.
  3. Submit:
    • Go to the submission page and paste all task links.
