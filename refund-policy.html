Cancellation & Refund Policy

Last Updated: January 2025

1. Overview

This policy outlines the conditions under which refunds or cancellations may be granted for services purchased from Alfido Tech.

2. Eligible Refund Cases

  • Duplicate or accidental payment
  • Technical payment failure
  • Service not initiated due to system error

3. Non-Refundable Cases

  • Completed or partially completed internships
  • Certificates already issued
  • Services already delivered
  • User dissatisfaction without valid reason

4. Refund Request Process

To request a refund, email us at support@alfidotech.com with the following details:

  • Full Name
  • Registered Email
  • Transaction ID
  • Payment Date
  • Reason for Refund

5. Timeframe

Refund requests must be submitted within 7 days of payment. Approved refunds will be processed within 7–10 business days.

6. Payment Method

Refunds will be credited to the original payment method only. No cash refunds will be issued.

7. Policy Updates

Alfido Tech reserves the right to update this policy at any time. Changes will be effective immediately upon posting.

8. Contact

For refund-related queries, contact:
📧 support@alfidotech.com
