1. The IT Industry in 2025 – What’s Changing?

The IT sector is moving at lightning speed. With modern tools, automation, and AI-driven development, companies are rewriting the rules of software development.

  • Shift to low-code and no-code platforms
  • AI-assisted debugging & auto code generation
  • Next-gen cloud-native app ecosystems
  • Full-stack developers becoming “AI-augmented engineers”

Enterprises aim for **scalable infrastructures**, **automation of workflows**, and **zero downtime deployment**. The demand for Cloud, Cybersecurity and AI skills is at an all-time high.

As businesses accelerate digital transformation, DevOps and cloud engineering are becoming core IT pillars. Tools such as Docker, Kubernetes, Terraform, and GitHub Actions rule modern pipelines.
